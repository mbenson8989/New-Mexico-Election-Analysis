## Vote-flip county graphics

**Only 3 of New Mexico’s 33 counties show a vote flip from 2022 to 2026\*.** A county counts as a vote flip only when its Democratic governor votes actually **fell** and its Republican governor votes **rose**. Statewide, Democratic governor votes **rose +46,204\***, and Democratic votes rose in 28 of 33 counties. No drop was invented; only real drops are shown as flips.

**Flips vs. surges.** A **flip** is a county where Democratic votes fell while Republican votes rose. A **surge** is the opposite kind of change: Democratic votes rose. To fill out a top ten, the other 7 cards show the 7 counties with the largest raw Democratic governor-vote gains from 2022 to 2026\* (not counting the flip counties or Guadalupe and Harding). Each surge card is marked **SURGE, NOT A FLIP** and uses a teal accent instead of the flip cards’ red and gold.

**Flip magnitude** = Democratic vote drop + Republican vote gain (2022 → 2026\*), in votes. That is the change in the Republican-minus-Democratic margin. Counties are ranked by it. (The Texas vote-flip cards used a different test, Democratic primary voters down while Democratic votes rose, so only their visual style is reused here.)

### Citation check

- **Executive Order 14399**, “Ensuring Citizenship Verification and Integrity in Federal Elections”: signed March 31, 2026; published at **91 FR 17125** (April 3, 2026, FR Doc. 2026-06601). [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/04/03/2026-06601/ensuring-citizenship-verification-and-integrity-in-federal-elections)
- Exact quote, Sec. 1: “The Federal Government has an unavoidable duty under Article II of the Constitution of the United States to enforce Federal law, which includes preventing violations of Federal criminal law and maintaining public confidence in election outcomes.”
- The order separately cites “the Federal Government’s constitutional obligation to guarantee a republican form of Government to every State in the Union, U.S. Const. Art. IV, Sec. 4” (the Guarantee Clause). The words “unavoidable duty” belong to the Article II sentence, not to the Guarantee Clause itself.
- Guarantee Clause text: [U.S. Constitution, Art. IV, Sec. 4](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/constitution-transcript).
